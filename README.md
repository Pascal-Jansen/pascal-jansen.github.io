### About Me

I am a **PhD candidate and Research Associate in Human-Computer Interaction (HCI)** at the Institute of Media Informatics, Ulm University, Germany.

My research lies at the intersection of human-computer interaction and **embodied AI**, such as automated vehicles and service robots.

My goal is embodied AI that people can accept and use safely. To get there, I develop and study interfaces that mediate what people perceive of the AI, so that AI behavior is appropriate to human experience and vice versa. I call this **calibrated human-AI interaction**.

To learn more about my work, please visit my [website](https://pascal-jansen.github.io/ "website").
